Notes:
  - total set discoveries and permuted-null counts are over the same multi-gene cluster universe.
  - nominal p<0.05 is the uncorrected upper reference, not a valid FDR control.
  - every cluster is tested against all ~896 sets (ora tests the full universe per group), so BH divides the
    best hit by that count; the strongest single hit anywhere is p=4.4e-04, giving min BH q=0.39.
  - Storey and two-stage BH do not help: a 2-8 gene cluster leaves almost all sets genuinely null (pi0~1),
    so the adaptive pi0 factor is near 1 and the adjusted values track plain BH.
  - CONCLUSION: on the broad net no within-cluster p-value correction recovers the enrichment, and nominal
    p<0.05 is not real signal (it also lights up the permuted null). The lever is the test universe, not the
    correction: restricting each cluster's tests to the sets its genes actually hit (conventional overlap-
    based ORA) or a curated collection is what recovers the paper's per-cluster count.
